# Interaction polish

Preserves the restored interface, layout, content and palette. Adds shared motion tokens, brief button press feedback, gentle pointer card lift, direction-aware row/arrow motion, dialog and backdrop entry, and a short cross-document fade. Light-mode shadows are adapted to their surfaces.

The public theme build bundles motion.css last, so timing stays consistent on every page without another browser request. Disabled controls do not move; coarse pointers suppress card/row hover movement. Both OS reduced motion and Portfolio's saved reduced-motion preference stop animation and displacement.

Verification: three public theme builds passed; Academy inventory/reference audit passed. Browser computed styles confirmed 160/240 ms control/card timing, 3 px card lift, 220 ms dialog/result entry and RTL -3 px row direction. Portfolio reduced-motion preference was tested through its settings and restored; transitions became 0 s.
