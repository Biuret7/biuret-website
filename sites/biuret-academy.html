OVERVIEW

Learning with a clearer path.

Biuret Academy turns a large field into an Arabic-first learning environment with local accounts, right-to-left interfaces, progress tracking, smart review, practical learning, and a next-lesson workflow.

99 structured lessons

Local safe labs, assessments, CTF-style challenges, and completion checks turn study into active practice.

Smart progress

Local SQLite progress, spaced review, a daily plan, and PDF section exports support sustained learning.

Focus: Building an Arabic cybersecurity learning experience that is structured, practical, and designed for consistent progress.
