CASE SUMMARY

Privacy with practical control.

BiuLock explores how a personal vault can remain useful without treating security as an invisible detail. The experience prioritizes local ownership, clear state, and recovery-aware decisions.

SELECTED HIGHLIGHTS

Built around everyday trust.

Organized private vault

Important records are kept in one focused workspace designed around privacy and clear organization.

Companion access

Desktop and mobile experiences are designed to work together while preserving deliberate user control.

Recovery-aware design

Backup and recovery flows are treated as core product experiences rather than last-minute utilities.
