OVERVIEW

Research with better structure.

B-Recon gives investigations a structured workspace: findings can be added to a case with scope notes, timestamps, and integrity hashes, then carried through review, reporting, and carefully protected export flows.

PROJECT SCOPE

From a pivot to a dossier.

Investigation cases

Case workspaces preserve evidence, scope notes, status, and an auditable timeline for focused research.

Research pivots

Dedicated modules support organized pivots across publicly available intelligence without losing context.

Evidence & reports

Professional PDF dossiers and encrypted case exports help retain integrity and portability where research is permitted.

Focus: Improving research clarity and evidence handling while keeping every workflow grounded in ethical, authorized use.
