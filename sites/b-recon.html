CONCEPT SUMMARY

Research with better structure.

B-Recon focuses on the work around research: defining scope, preserving context, organizing findings, and turning scattered observations into a clear case narrative.

PLANNED FEATURES

From a lead to a clear report.

Case organization

Planned capability: Separate workspaces keep scope, findings, and research context connected throughout an investigation.

Guided research

Planned capability: Selected workflows help researchers move between public information sources without losing the larger context.

Evidence & reporting

Planned capability: Findings can be reviewed and prepared as concise outputs for responsible, authorized use.

THE USER JOURNEY

A useful problem comes first.

Who would use it?

Students and authorized researchers who need to keep public-source research organized around a defined case.

The problem to solve

Links and observations lose their context when scattered across tabs. Source, date and uncertainty need to stay attached to each claim.

A proposed everyday flow

Define the scope, record public sources, distinguish observations from conclusions and assemble a traceable report.

NEXT IMPLEMENTATION STEPS

A plan to validate, then build.

Proposed milestones, not completed deliverables.

  1. Design the case and evidence model

    Describe scope, provenance, timestamps and confidence before adding collection tools.

  2. Build a local research workspace

    Start with manual evidence capture and a readable case timeline rather than unsupported automatic conclusions.

  3. Review traceability and privacy

    Verify source links, export accuracy and data handling using consented sample cases.

Project status: This page is a future product plan. Repository code is experimental and does not represent a released, validated, or deployment-ready application.
