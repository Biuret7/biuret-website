ABOUT BIURET

Curiosity, turned into working projects.

Biuret is a cybersecurity-focused developer portfolio shaped by practical learning. The work here spans security, OSINT, reverse engineering, Linux, programming, and educational tools.

This portfolio is intentionally alive: concepts become experiments, experiments become projects, and each project documents the next area to explore.
